# Market Maker discovery priority

The broad event scan initially returned distant 2030–2045 contracts. Several had substantial contract volume but too few actual public trades to qualify. CPU-driven admission pauses made reaching later event pages especially slow.

After its first broad page, discovery refreshes a volume-ranked series catalog using category queries at most once per category per hour. It retains up to twelve eligible series from each of the four categories (48 IDs), interleaves categories and directs three of every four event-page requests to that pool. Every fourth request advances the independent broad cursor. Each series has an independent cursor; state survives worker recovery.

This replaces the existing event-page request rather than adding per-variant scans. The added budget is at most six category catalog reads per hour (one per pass during the initial refresh); candidate trade/depth verification and shared subscription limits remain unchanged. Closed, short-duration, crypto and weather contracts remain excluded. Historical series volume is a discovery prior only: current public-trade frequency, live spread, depth and fee checks still determine admission. No orders are generated by the catalog alone.

Catalog failure falls back to broad pagination. Calendar, portfolio, risk and resource gates remain in force. No paid resource change, data reset or real-money permissions are involved.

## Response-size recovery

Discovery retains the transport's 4 MiB response limit. Nested event pages start at 50 events and halve after an oversized response. Failed reads count against discovery statistics; the cursor stays unchanged. When a single event exceeds the limit, discovery reads that event's header and pages its markets separately. Its independent market cursor and the next event cursor survive checkpoint recovery. The broad or series cursor advances only after that event's market pages complete.

GET /series is explicitly permitted by the GET-only adapter. Catalogues use category filters rather than fetching the entire catalogue. A category failure does not disable the other categories. Each category query is attempted at most once per hour, with at most one category catalogue request per discovery pass. Scanner logs include capacity pauses, page size, catalogue state, and independently verified trade counts.

## Oversized Sports catalogue fallback

A public read on November 8, 2026 verified that the Football-tagged Sports catalogue returned 604 series in approximately 0.98 MB, below the unchanged 4 MiB cap. If the full Sports catalogue fails, one conditional Football-tagged catalogue query is allowed per hour, still at most one catalogue query per pass. The total maximum is seven catalogue queries per hour. The tagged query uses the same ranking, current-trade verification and shared stream as other candidates.

The futures classifier includes championships, conferences/divisions, season awards, playoff qualification, trophies and Super Bowl futures. It excludes game, half, quarter, period, inning, spread, touchdown and yardage series, including misleading first-half and quarter names containing “winner.” A bare unclassified “winner” is not sufficient futures evidence. Minimum remaining time, current liquidity, executed-trade counts and execution economics remain required.
