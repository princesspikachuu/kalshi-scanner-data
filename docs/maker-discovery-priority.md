# Market Maker discovery priority

The broad event scan initially returned distant 2030–2045 contracts. Several had substantial contract volume but too few actual public trades to qualify. CPU-driven admission pauses made reaching later event pages especially slow.

After its first broad page, discovery refreshes a volume-ranked series catalog at most once per hour. It retains up to twelve eligible series from each of the four categories (48 IDs), interleaves categories and directs three of every four event-page requests to that pool. Every fourth request advances the independent broad cursor. Each series has an independent cursor; state survives worker recovery.

This replaces the existing event-page request rather than adding per-variant scans. The added budget is one catalog read per hour; candidate trade/depth verification and shared subscription limits remain unchanged. Closed, short-duration, crypto and weather contracts remain excluded. Historical series volume is a discovery prior only: current public-trade frequency, live spread, depth and fee checks still determine admission. No orders are generated by the catalog alone.

Catalog failure falls back to broad pagination. Calendar, portfolio, risk and resource gates remain in force. No paid resource change, data reset or real-money permissions are involved.
