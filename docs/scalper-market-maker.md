# Paper execution upgrade and Market Maker

The existing three scalper wallets, profiles, attempts and accumulated research are retained. Additive, idempotent installers introduce independent Momentum and Liquidity Dislocation scalper wallets and two Market Maker wallets (baseline and competitive). Existing High Probability, Sequential Spread, Complete Set and Convexity execution remains unchanged. Every new portfolio starts with independent virtual funds; no exchange order-writing path is introduced.

## Execution and accounting

Shared causal two-second microstructure frames have at most 60 prices and 256 deduplicated public trades per market. Book sequencing, missing-update detection, fresh full snapshots and current fee verification continue through the existing execution infrastructure. Positions react to incoming events; fresh entries use bounded clock frames. Depth estimates inspect at most 20 levels. Maker/taker selection accounts for fees, depth, observed movement and explicitly uncalibrated queue/flow estimates. Estimated fill probability is a ranking input, never fill authorization.

Maker submissions arrive after modeled transport and worker delays. Resting buys and covered sales require subsequent aggressive trades that clear modeled displayed queue plus seeded 5–35% additional competition, or trade through the limit. Shared print quantity cannot be spent twice within a portfolio. Deduplication retains only the live timestamp window, with a fail-closed 32,768-record capacity; long-dated flat probes are removed from memory after rotation while journal/research and financial summaries are preserved. Repricing loses queue priority. Cancellation remains pending during simulated latency and keeps reservations until acknowledgement. IOC orders consume available portfolio-local depth; changing or missing liquidity can cause partial fills or no fill. Exact exchange queue priority cannot be reconstructed from aggregated books.

Covered sales reserve existing inventory, not cash or complementary contracts. Recovery still recognizes legacy complementary maker exit orders. Actual quantities, fees and wallet movements determine realized P/L. Current scalper/maker value uses visible executable bids less estimated taker exit fees; insufficient depth uses an explicitly estimated residual mark, and stale books use cached verified bids or entry cost. Valuation never manufactures a sale. Unliquidated inventory remains pending until an authoritative settlement result.

Sizing includes outstanding reservations and held cost at portfolio, category, event and market scope. Normal, reduced-holding, restricted-entry and liquidation phases use actual close timestamps and category-specific parameters. Protective exits remain active during entry pauses or processing backpressure. Each approach retains independent causal observations and financial accounting; no automatic optimization is enabled.

## Market Maker discovery and risk

Only Politics, Economics / Finance, Sports Futures and Entertainment / Culture are eligible. Crypto, weather, hourly and sports games are excluded. Baseline admission starts at 24 hours remaining, 20–80% contract price, 3-cent spread and 30 executed trades in 24 hours; the competitive wallet compares 2-cent spreads. A broader bounded near-miss pool is visible without committing capital. Trading volume is only a cheap prefilter: actual public trade records verify frequency before admission. Displayed depth must support intended size, and unstable trade ranges are rejected.

Discovery uses one events page and checks at most one candidate's trade history and three-level book every 30 seconds, through the existing shared three-reads/second budget. At most two distinct maker markets use spare monitoring capacity. Existing strategies and protected positions are not evicted. Scalper clock passes visit only owners of admitted markets. Buffered bursts amortize persistence over at most 256 inputs or 40ms of simulation before yielding; normal batches retain the existing 128-input/20ms ceiling. Event order and atomic financial checkpoints are preserved. Backpressure stops admission and fresh entries when projected queue-clearing time exceeds 0.5 seconds, measured lag exceeds 2 seconds, backlog exceeds 2,000 events, processing exceeds the existing cost ceiling, or read cooldown is active. Fast short bursts are not classified as overload from count alone. Capacity growth still requires sustained healthy measurements; inactive flat candidates rotate. Known settlement requests have their own bounded cadence. Incentive metadata is refreshed at most hourly, with reward estimates disabled and incentives excluded from P/L.

Category profiles and individual admitted markets can pause entries independently while inventory exits continue. Inventory blocks additional linked YES/NO entries; event/category limits constrain correlated contracts. Adverse momentum, aggressive trade pressure and liquidity withdrawal cancel vulnerable quotes. Maximum holding times, price stops, trailing exits and scheduled-event windows reduce inventory. Small quote changes do not immediately discard queue priority.

Schedules come only from published exchange milestones explicitly linked to the event. This does not provide comprehensive news, injury, court or macro calendar coverage. Missing schedules are identified, and market-data protections remain active. Liquidity-program metadata is best effort and limited to the first published page; no eligibility or rewards are promised.

## Reporting and operational verification

The dashboard retains existing collapsible folders and mobile cards. Category evidence pools YES/NO by entry contract percentage and remaining time. Per-trade direction remains available for audit. New research includes attempts, submissions, partial fills, cancellation/repricing, risk/economic rejection reasons, round trips, holding/wait times, spread capture, maker/taker results, settlement carryovers and utilization. Existing compressed minute histories carry cumulative research counters, allowing 1h, 6h, 24h, 7d and 30d differences without another persistence loop. Window boundaries have existing minute sampling precision; absent historical research baselines are unavailable rather than invented. Category cells and the latest 20 round-trip details are lifetime evidence; complete execution history remains in the durable journal.

Operational logs include `market_maker_scan`, `market_maker_retry`, `market_maker_health`, `volatility_scalper_health`, `paper_monitoring_capacity`, `shadow_pipeline_cost`, and existing financial samples. A healthy deployment must show actual fresh books, opportunities and submitted orders, then subsequent real trade-driven fills or explicit queue/liquidity misses. Neither passing fixtures nor observed quotes establish profitable live performance.

Fees reuse verified series/event schedules and the published Kalshi formulas with centicent cost-plus-fee rounding. Official references: https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf and https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/orderbook_responses .
