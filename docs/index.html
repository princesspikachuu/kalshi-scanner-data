What am I looking at?

The large number in each square is the average simulated profit or loss per contract. For example, +4.2¢ means that group gained an average of 4.2 cents per hypothetically filled contract before fees. A loss of −8¢ means it lost an average of eight cents.

Tap any populated square to see its number of wins, average bid, ROI, and days represented. Comparing rows is exploratory: repeated observations of the same contracts are not independent trades.
